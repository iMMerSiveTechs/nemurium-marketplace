SKILL

Architecture Decision Records

Systematically document technical trade-offs, technology choices, and their long-term consequences. Transform architectural discussions into living records that guide future decisions and onboard new team members.

Core Capabilities

📋

Structured ADR Templates

Standardized format capturing problem statement, decision rationale, alternatives considered, and future implications. Ensures consistency across your architecture knowledge base.

⚖️

Trade-off Analysis

Systematically evaluate competing options against documented criteria. Cost, performance, maintainability, scalability, and operational complexity all weighed transparently.

🔗

Dependency Mapping

Understand which decisions impact others. Visualize how architectural choices compound and affect downstream systems, preventing isolated decisions that create technical debt.

📚

Decision Context Preservation

Capture why a choice was made at a specific point in time. Preserves business context, constraints, and team knowledge that would otherwise vanish with team turnover.

🔄

Evolution Tracking

Decisions change. Track when choices become obsolete, what caused reversals, and how architectures evolved. Learn from past decisions to improve future ones.

🎯

Consequence Documentation

Link decisions to their measurable outcomes. Did performance improve? What new constraints emerged? Connect theory to reality with documented consequences and learned lessons.

Real-World Applications

🏢 Large Team Coordination

25+ engineers making divergent architectural choices. ADRs establish shared principles and prevent duplicated decisions across teams working on database selection, service boundaries, and deployment strategies.

🚀 Scaling & Refactoring

Justify why monolith-to-microservices transition happened. Document which components were decoupled, why certain services were kept together, and what assumptions guided the migration.

👥 Onboarding New Engineers

Instead of "that's just how we do it," new team members read the full story. They understand technology choices, historical context, and constraints that shaped your system's current architecture.

🔀 Technology Migrations

Planning to move from REST to gRPC? Upgrade database engines? Document current decision, alternatives evaluated, and criteria for success. Create continuity for multi-quarter migrations.
