Draft Offer

Generate professional offer letters in minutes with built-in total comp packages, equity modeling, and negotiation guidance. Stay compliant across jurisdictions while empowering candidates with transparent compensation details.

Core Capabilities

Offer Letter Templates

Pre-built, legally-reviewed templates for every role type and level. Customize job title, reporting structure, start date, and employment terms in seconds.

Total Comp Packaging

Visualize full compensation packages including base salary, bonus targets, equity grants, PTO, benefits, and signing bonuses. Show candidates the real value proposition.

Equity Modeling

Calculate vesting schedules, grant dates, and dilution impacts. Model different equity offers (options, RSUs, profit interests) with scenario planning.

Negotiation Guidance

Arm your recruiters and hiring managers with negotiation scripts and counter-offer playbooks. Know what room you have before the conversation starts.

Multi-Jurisdiction Compliance

Automatically adjust for state and local employment laws, wage transparency requirements, and payroll tax implications across the US and beyond.

Signature & Tracking

Send offers via secure e-signature, track acceptance rates, and log all versioning. Get executive dashboards on offer status and time-to-acceptance.

Common Use Cases

Competitive Offer Battles

When top candidates have competing offers, quickly model equity, signing bonus, and accelerated review options. Respond in hours, not days.

Startup & Hypergrowth

Model option pools, secondary sales, and liquidation scenarios. Show candidates realistic upside while being transparent about risk.

International Expansion

Generate compliant offer letters for multiple countries. Incorporate local benefits, statutory holidays, visa sponsorship, and relocation packages.

Offer Acceptance Optimization

Track which compensation packages drive acceptance. A/B test sign-on bonuses, equity, and benefits to improve close rates.
