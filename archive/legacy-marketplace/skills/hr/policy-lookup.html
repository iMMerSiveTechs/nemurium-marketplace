SKILL

Policy Lookup

Centralize your company policies in plain-English summaries that employees and managers actually understand. Make policies searchable, versionable, and accessible via mobile so everyone knows the rules.

Core Capabilities

Policy Library

Centralized repository of all company policies: PTO, remote work, expenses, harassment, code of conduct, benefits, parental leave. Version control tracks changes and who made them.

Plain-Language Summaries

Translate dense policy documents into employee-friendly summaries. Use examples, FAQs, and decision trees to make policies approachable and understandable.

Search & Navigation

Smart search across policies with autocomplete and tag-based filtering. Employees find answers without digging through handbooks. Category browsing for exploratory learning.

Acknowledgment Tracking

Require employees to acknowledge policy understanding. Track acceptance by employee and policy. Maintain compliance records for audits.

Manager Guidance

Provide managers with decision frameworks for common policy questions: can I approve remote work? How do I handle PTO requests? When should I escalate to HR?

Audit & Compliance

Log all policy access and acknowledgments. Generate compliance reports for regulatory reviews. Track policy updates and who approved them.

Common Use Cases

Onboarding New Employees

Guide new hires through company policies during onboarding. Require acknowledgment to ensure they understand remote work, expenses, and time-off policies.

Manager Self-Service

Help managers answer policy questions without escalating to HR. Provide decision trees for common scenarios: Can they work from home? How much PTO can they take?

Policy Updates & Communications

When policies change, notify affected employees and require re-acknowledgment. Log who was notified and who acknowledged the changes.

Compliance & Dispute Resolution

When disputes arise, prove employees were informed of policies. Show signed acknowledgments and access logs to support your defense.
