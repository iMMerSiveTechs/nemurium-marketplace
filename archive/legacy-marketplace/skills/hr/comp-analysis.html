SKILL

Comp Analysis

Build a data-driven compensation strategy with real-time market benchmarking, salary band placement, equity modeling, and retention risk scoring. Ensure pay equity, attract talent, and optimize your talent budget.

Core Capabilities

Market Benchmarking

Real-time salary data from Radford, SHRM, PayScale, and custom surveys. Filter by function, level, geography, company size, and industry to get accurate market bands for every role.

Salary Band Management

Define competitive salary ranges with 25th, 50th, and 75th percentile guidance. Set minimums, midpoints, and maximums that align with business strategy and market reality.

Position Leveling

Compare internal roles against market data to identify over-paid and underpaid positions. Right-size your org structure and compensation for competitiveness.

Equity Modeling

Model equity grants across levels and scenarios. Analyze fully diluted value, vesting cliff impact, and total comp positioning. Scenario-plan for different funding outcomes.

Pay Equity Audits

Analyze pay by tenure, performance, demographics, and department. Spot unexplained pay gaps and generate remediation plans. Stay defensible for regulatory review.

Retention Risk Scoring

Identify high-flight-risk employees based on tenure, pay vs. market, performance, and engagement. Proactively model retention impacts and counter-offer economics.

Common Use Cases

Annual Comp Review & Planning

Design salary ranges, plan merit increases, and set equity grants for the upcoming year. Model comp scenarios against headcount plans and budget forecasts.

Retention During Economic Uncertainty

Know which employees are most at-risk of leaving for higher pay. Model counter-offer economics and decide where to invest in retention.

Equity Pay Compliance

Audit gender, race, and age pay gaps. Document findings and build remediation plans to stay compliant with state and federal pay transparency laws.

Geographic Expansion

Benchmark new market entry salaries. Build competitive salary bands for Austin, London, or Singapore. Understand cost-of-living adjustments and talent premiums.
