Runbook

Every incident, maintenance window, and recurring task deserves a playbook. Step-by-step commands, decision trees, troubleshooting paths, and escalation protocols all in one place. When crises hit at 3am, your runbook is the truth.

Core Capabilities

What you can do with Runbook

📋

Step-by-Step Procedures

Break complex tasks into numbered steps. Include expected outputs, timing, and who should perform each action.

🛠️

Command Snippets

Copy-paste ready commands. Include prerequisites, parameters, expected results. Works offline when you need it most.

🌳

Decision Trees

If X happens, do Y. If Y fails, try Z. Branch logic so anyone can resolve issues without guessing.

🚨

Escalation Paths

When to page the on-call engineer. Who has the keys. How to declare a SEV-1. Emergency contacts and war rooms.

⚠️

Troubleshooting Guides

Symptom-based navigation. "App is slow" → Check CPU → Check memory → Check DB connections. Fast diagnosis.

📞

Contact & Dependency Maps

Who owns what system. Slack channels, phone numbers, escalation contacts. Know who to call instantly.
