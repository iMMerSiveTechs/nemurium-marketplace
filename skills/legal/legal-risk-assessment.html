SKILL

Legal Risk Assessment

Quantify legal exposure using a severity-by-likelihood framework. Get risk scores, escalation criteria, and recommended mitigation strategies for any legal issue.

Core Capabilities

📊

Risk Quantification

Apply severity-by-likelihood framework to any legal issue. Get numerical risk scores (1-10) that translate legal concerns into business decision metrics.

🎯

Severity Scoring

Assess financial impact, reputational damage, operational disruption, and regulatory consequence. Weight factors by importance to your organization.

📈

Likelihood Assessment

Estimate probability of adverse outcome based on jurisdiction, precedent, facts, and counterparty behavior. Context-aware predictions informed by case law.

🚨

Escalation Criteria

Auto-flag risks that exceed your tolerance thresholds. Route to GC, CFO, or board based on exposure size and probability. Define escalation rules once.

💡

Mitigation Strategy

For high-risk items, get recommended mitigation approaches. Negotiate terms, obtain insurance, restructure deals, or escalate to leadership.

📋

Risk Register

Build and maintain risk register. Track exposure over time. Document decisions. Prepare audit trail for board and investors.

Common Use Cases

📋 Deal Committee Review

M&A, partnership, or major contract on the table? Get quantified legal risk score. Present to deal committee with severity and likelihood breakdown. Make better decisions.

🏢 Litigation Risk Assessment

Demand letter or threatened suit? Assess likelihood of adverse judgment and likely damages. Inform settlement strategy. Know when to fight versus when to settle.

🔍 Compliance Issue Triage

Potential regulatory violation identified. Score severity and enforcement likelihood. Decide whether to self-report, remediate quietly, or escalate to board.

📊 Risk Portfolio Management

Portfolio of known legal risks across the org. Assess each one. Rank by severity. Allocate resources to highest-impact mitigation efforts.
