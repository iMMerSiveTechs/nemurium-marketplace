Ticket Triage

Instantly categorize incoming support tickets, assign P1-P4 priority levels, route to the right team, and identify duplicates or known issues—all automatically. Reduce MTTR and increase first-contact resolution.

Key Capabilities

⚡

Smart Categorization

Automatically classify tickets by issue type: billing, technical, account, feature request, or complaint—using context and language analysis.

📊

Priority Assignment

Assign P1–P4 priority levels based on customer tier, issue severity, and business impact to optimize queue management.

🔄

Duplicate Detection

Identify duplicate tickets and link to existing issues, preventing wasted effort and keeping conversations unified.

🎯

Intelligent Routing

Auto-route tickets to the right team or specialist based on category, priority, and workload distribution.

🧠

Known Issue Matching

Cross-reference against your KB and issue tracker to flag recurring problems and suggest documented solutions.

📝

Summary Generation

Generate concise ticket summaries for faster context capture and better team communication on handoff.

Common Use Cases

High-Volume Support Teams

Triage hundreds of tickets daily without manual review. Ensure P1 incidents surface instantly and reach the right engineer in seconds.

Multi-Channel Support Centers

Unified triage for email, chat, forms, and social media. One standard categorization across all channels reduces confusion.

Distributed Support Teams

Distribute tickets fairly across global teams based on expertise and workload. No more bottlenecks or missed escalations.

SLA-Critical Environments

Enforce consistent P1-P4 assignment tied to SLA timers. Automatically flag at-risk tickets before deadlines.
