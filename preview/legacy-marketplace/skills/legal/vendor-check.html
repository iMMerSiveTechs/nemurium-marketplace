Vendor Check

Consolidate vendor agreements scattered across CLM, CRM, email, and storage systems. Run gap analysis, flag missing terms, identify renegotiation opportunities.

Core Capabilities

📦

Agreement Consolidation

Pull vendor agreements from CLM, CRM, email archives, and cloud storage. Consolidate master documents into single vendor record. Know what you've signed.

🔗

Contract Linking

Automatically identify related amendments, SOWs, exhibits, and side letters. Assemble complete contract package. Know the full scope of each vendor relationship.

🎯

Gap Analysis

Compare vendor agreements against your standards. Flag missing terms: IP indemnification, data security, liability cap, termination for convenience, audit rights.

⚠️

Risk Identification

Highlight problematic terms: unlimited liability, perpetual confidentiality, one-sided indemnification, poor termination rights, restrictive payment terms.

📅

Renewal Tracking

Extract renewal dates, termination windows, and auto-renewal provisions. Calendar upcoming deadlines. Flag renegotiation opportunities before renewal.

💰

Cost Analysis

Extract pricing, payment terms, annual cost, and volume discounts. Benchmark against peers. Identify cost reduction or volume leverage opportunities.

Common Use Cases

🏢 Vendor Portfolio Audit

How many vendors does your company actually have? What terms did we agree to? Consolidate portfolio, assess risk, identify cost savings and renegotiation targets.

🔄 Renewal Negotiation

Vendor renewal coming up in 3 months. What did we commit to? What gaps exist in our contract? Build renegotiation strategy before renewal window opens.

🌍 Vendor Consolidation

Multiple business units have separate agreements with same vendor. Consolidate and renegotiate as single contract. Increase leverage. Reduce cost.

⚖️ Compliance & Audit

Auditor asks for all vendor agreements with specific clause (e.g., data security, audit rights). Search across all vendors. Assemble responsive set instantly.
