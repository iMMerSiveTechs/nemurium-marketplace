Daily Briefing

Start every morning with a prioritized sales briefing. Key deals at risk, opportunities to advance, important customer updates, and action items—all in one place.

What You Can Do

📌

Prioritized Deal Summary

Get a real-time snapshot of your pipeline. High-priority deals, stage changes, stalled opportunities, and immediate action items ranked by impact.

⚠️

Risk Flags & Alerts

Automatic detection of at-risk deals. No activity in 14 days? Prospect went quiet? Key stakeholder left the company? Get alerted immediately.

🎯

Today's Action Items

Automated list of calls to make, emails to send, and follow-ups due today. Prioritized by deal value and probability of close.

💬

Customer Updates & News

Monitor for customer company news, funding rounds, leadership changes, and growth signals. Stay ahead of upsell and churn risk.

📊

Weekly & Monthly Metrics

Track your pipeline velocity, win rate, average deal size, and forecast vs. actuals. Know how you're trending toward your number.

📧

Delivered via Email or Slack

Get your briefing daily at your preferred time via email or Slack. Read in 5 minutes and stay focused on what matters most.

Real-World Use Cases

☀️ Morning Standup Ready

Before you join your team standup, you've already reviewed your priorities, flagged issues, and identified the day's critical actions. Lead the meeting with confidence.

🚨 Early Warning System

Prospect silence shows up as a risk flag in your briefing. You reach out proactively before the deal goes cold. Early intervention saves the deal.

📈 Forecast Accountability

Sales VP gets a daily briefing on the entire team's pipeline. Spot team performance trends, allocate coaching where needed, adjust forecast with confidence.

🎯 Territory Optimization

New rep inherits territory. Daily briefing shows them what to focus on, which accounts have the most opportunity, and what's at risk of being lost.
