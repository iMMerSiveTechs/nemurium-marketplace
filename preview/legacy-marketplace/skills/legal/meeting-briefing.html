Meeting Briefing

Generate concise legal briefings for contract negotiations, board meetings, and compliance reviews. Include key decisions, action items, and escalation points—tracked and actionable.

Core Capabilities

📄

Executive Briefing

Input meeting context (negotiation, board, compliance). Get concise 1-pager with legal issues, key decisions needed, and recommended positions. Executive-ready.

🎯

Key Decision Identification

Automatically extract what decisions the meeting needs to make. Flag decisions that have legal implications. Separate negotiable from non-negotiable.

✅

Action Item Tracking

Log action items and owners during or after meeting. Attach deadlines and escalation criteria. Auto-send reminders to responsible parties.

🔐

Negotiation Strategy

For contract negotiations, get GC-recommended positions on each open term. Include fallback positions and walk-away thresholds for each issue.

📊

Risk Briefing

For board and risk committee meetings, present legal risk register. Highlight emerging issues. Summarize mitigation efforts and recommended actions.

📝

Meeting Minutes

Capture meeting notes. Auto-generate meeting minutes with key decisions, action items, and owners. Maintain audit trail of board and leadership decisions.

Common Use Cases

🤝 Contract Negotiation

Upcoming meeting with vendor or customer to negotiate terms. Get briefing with our positions on price, liability, IP, data, and compliance terms. Know what to ask for.

📊 Board Meeting

Quarterly or special board meeting. Get legal briefing on critical issues, pending litigation, compliance status, and board decisions needed. Present with confidence.

✅ Compliance Review

Audit or compliance review meeting coming up. Summarize compliance status, outstanding issues, remediation efforts, and timeline for resolution.

⚖️ Litigation Strategy

Case strategy meeting with outside counsel. Prepare briefing on legal claims, defense strategy, risk assessment, and settlement considerations.
