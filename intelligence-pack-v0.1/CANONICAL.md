# Canonical Understanding — Nemurium Decision Engine

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Status:** Reality v0 map. North Star is labeled as destination.

## The one-line thesis

Nemurium is a decision engine that turns market signals into trusted action, and captures the reasoning so the next decision is better.

Not five products. One machine with five faces.

## What we sell (and what we do not)

Do not sell: "AI agents." That market is crowded and cancel-prone.

Sell: **the operating intelligence layer that turns AI capability into trusted execution.**

The wedge is accountability. The product is capability + simplicity + harness power + full scope.

| Audience | Door | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Origin + HOH | Deploy AI agents you can trust. |
| Builders | HOH | Build AI systems without rebuilding the infrastructure. |
| Everyday users | Browser | Your AI workspace that can actually do things. |

## Two views (keep them separate)

North Star: World signals → Magazine → GlassGraph → HOH → Browser → Origin receipts → learning.

Reality v0: signals → human research + AI assistance → Magazine issue → decision records → manual graph updates → future automation.

The flywheel is the destination. The first version is a manually operated intelligence loop.

## Circuit

dot → slice → map → decision → HOH → browser → Origin receipt → memory → next dot

Nerves: GlassGraph (relationships) and HOH (authority). Spine: Origin. Public face: Magazine.

Do not prove you can build an AI operating system. Prove you can understand the AI operating system market better than everyone else. Issue Zero is that proof.
