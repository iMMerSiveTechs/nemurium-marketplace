Research Synthesis

Transform raw research data into strategic insights. Extract themes, identify patterns, and synthesize actionable recommendations from interviews, surveys, usability tests, and ethnographic studies. Get clear narrative of your users' needs, pain points, and opportunities—with specificity that drives product decisions.

What You Get

Qualitative Theme Extraction

Systematic thematic analysis of interview transcripts and open-ended responses. Identifies recurring patterns, contradictions, and edge cases. Organized by user segment and behavior type.

User Journey Mapping

Multi-dimensional journey maps showing user context, emotions, and pain points at each stage. Identifies moments of opportunity and failure modes that matter most.

Quantitative Pattern Analysis

Survey data synthesis with frequency analysis, correlation identification, and demographic breakdown. Validates qualitative themes with statistical evidence.

User Persona Development

Evidence-based personas grounded in actual research data, not assumptions. Each persona includes motivations, constraints, and jobs-to-be-done aligned with findings.

Insights & Opportunity Gap

Clear articulation of customer needs alongside competitive landscape. Identifies white space opportunities and areas of unmet customer demand.

Recommendation Roadmap

Prioritized strategic recommendations with supporting evidence. Each recommendation tied directly to research findings with expected impact quantification.
