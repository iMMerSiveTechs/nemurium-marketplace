SKILL

Onboarding

Orchestrate your new hire experience from offer acceptance through first 90 days. Pre-start task lists, first-week agendas, manager coaching, and milestone check-ins ensure retention, ramp speed, and cultural integration.

Core Capabilities

Pre-Start Task Management

Auto-generate checklists for IT provisioning, facilities setup, background checks, and compliance training. Track completion and escalate blockers before the hire's first day.

First-Week Onboarding Plans

Hour-by-hour agendas with meet-and-greets, system access walkthroughs, team introductions, and lunch buddies. Make day one less overwhelming, more welcoming.

Manager Onboarding Coaching

Guide managers through the first 1:1s, goal setting, culture conversation, and feedback conversations. Equip them to set their new hire up for success.

30/60/90-Day Milestones

Structured milestone check-ins with competency mapping, learning plans, and feedback loops. Track ramp progress and adjust support as needed.

Buddy & Mentor Programs

Match new hires with peer mentors, peers from their background, or learning buddies. Accelerate cultural integration and informal knowledge transfer.

Engagement Surveys

Pulse checks at day 7, day 30, day 60, and day 90. Spot early flight risk, culture gaps, and onboarding friction points before they cause attrition.

Common Use Cases

High-Volume Onboarding (50+ hires/month)

Standardize onboarding across multiple roles, locations, and teams. Reduce IT bottlenecks and ensure new hires get consistent experiences despite scale.

Remote & Distributed Teams

Asynchronous onboarding, time-zone-friendly buddy matching, and virtual culture building. Make remote hires feel integrated despite never meeting in person.

Reducing First-Year Attrition

Most departures happen in the first 90 days. Use engagement surveys and manager touchpoints to spot disengagement early and intervene.

Ramp Acceleration

Track milestone completion and skill acquisition. Identify which onboarding patterns correlate with faster productivity and replicate them.
