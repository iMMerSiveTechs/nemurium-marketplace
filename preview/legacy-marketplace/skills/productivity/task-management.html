SKILL

Task Management

Manage your entire commitment portfolio in a single TASKS.md file. Add, prioritize, complete, and track every task with full context and dependencies.

Core Capabilities

➕

Add Tasks

Create tasks with context, deadlines, dependencies, and custom metadata. Bulk import from email, Slack, or create manually.

⚖️

Prioritize

Rank tasks by impact, urgency, and effort. Automatic dependency ordering ensures you work on blockers first.

✓

Complete & Track

Mark tasks complete with notes and evidence. Track completion rate and velocity over time.

🔗

Dependencies

Define task relationships and blockers. System automatically reorders work based on dependencies.

📊

Burndown & Velocity

Track sprint progress, completion rate, and forecast when key work will be done.

🔄

Recurring Tasks

Set up daily, weekly, or monthly tasks that auto-generate and stay on your list.

Typical Use Cases

Sprint Planning

Define sprint scope, estimate effort, and track progress toward completion within the sprint window.

Personal OKRs

Break OKRs into executable tasks and track progress against quarterly commitments.

Team Coordination

Share task lists with team members and track handoffs and dependencies.

Inbox Zero

Convert every commitment from email, chat, or conversation into a tracked task.
