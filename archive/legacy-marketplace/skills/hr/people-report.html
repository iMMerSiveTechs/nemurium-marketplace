SKILL

People Report

Visualize organizational health with real-time dashboards covering headcount, attrition, diversity, engagement, and flight risk. Give leadership the metrics they need to make informed people decisions.

Core Capabilities

Headcount Dashboards

Real-time visibility into current headcount by department, level, location, and status. Track open requisitions, time-to-fill, and hiring pipeline against targets.

Attrition Analytics

Measure voluntary and involuntary attrition by cohort, tenure, department, and manager. Benchmark against industry norms. Identify departments with high turnover.

Diversity Metrics

Track representation by gender, ethnicity, age, and disability status across levels. Set diversity goals and monitor progress. Compare hiring, promotion, and attrition rates by demographic.

Engagement & Survey Analytics

Visualize engagement scores, eNPS, and sentiment trends. Drill down by team, role, and manager. Correlate engagement with attrition and performance.

Flight Risk Scoring

Identify high-risk employees using tenure, engagement, performance, and compensation signals. Generate targeted retention lists and intervention recommendations.

Custom Reports & Exports

Build custom people dashboards for executives, department heads, and board reporting. Schedule automated reports and export data for further analysis.

Common Use Cases

Board & Investor Reporting

Create executive dashboards on headcount burn, attrition, and diversity. Show board members the health of your people organization at a glance.

Department Head Updates

Give managers visibility into their team's metrics: headcount vs. plan, attrition trends, engagement scores. Facilitate accountability and continuous improvement.

Predictive Attrition Campaigns

Use flight risk models to identify flight risks before they leave. Execute targeted retention campaigns for high-value employees at risk.

Diversity & Inclusion Tracking

Measure progress toward diversity goals. Analyze representation at each level and major department. Inform recruitment and promotion strategies with data.
