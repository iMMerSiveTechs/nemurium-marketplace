Design Critique

Transform feedback into better products. Structured design critique covering usability, visual hierarchy, consistency, and interaction patterns. Get expert perspective on design decisions—not opinions. Understand why something works or doesn't, with specific, actionable recommendations backed by design principles.

What You Get

Usability Evaluation

Deep analysis of user flows, task completion patterns, and potential friction points. Identifies where users might get stuck and why, with specific interaction improvements.

Visual Hierarchy Analysis

Examines information architecture, contrast, sizing, and spacing. Ensures critical information is discoverable and supporting information doesn't distract from primary actions.

Design System Consistency

Audits adherence to design tokens, component usage, and pattern consistency. Identifies instances of inconsistency that undermine user confidence and increase cognitive load.

Interaction Critique

Evaluates animation timing, transition logic, loading states, and feedback mechanisms. Ensures interactions feel intentional, responsive, and communicate system status effectively.

Comparative Analysis

Contextualizes your design against competitive products and industry standards. Identifies opportunity gaps and design leadership opportunities.

Priority-Ordered Recommendations

Clear guidance on which improvements deliver maximum user impact. Strategic roadmap separating quick wins from systemic improvements.
