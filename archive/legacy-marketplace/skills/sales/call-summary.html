Call Summary

Turn call recordings and notes into actionable outcomes. Extract key discussion points, generate follow-up emails, create CRM records, and build consensus—automatically.

What You Can Do

🎤

Audio to Transcript

Upload or link your Zoom/Teams/Slack recording and automatically generate an accurate transcript. Works with multiple speakers and background noise.

✅

Smart Action Items

Automatically extract action items with ownership, deadlines, and dependencies. Never lose track of commitments made during the call.

💌

Follow-Up Email Generation

Generate personalized follow-up emails with key discussion points, next steps, and value summary. Copy, customize, and send in seconds.

📊

Deal Health Assessment

Analyze call sentiment, engagement level, and buying signals. Get a quick health score for pipeline management and risk identification.

🔗

CRM Auto-Sync

Push summary, action items, and next steps directly into Salesforce, HubSpot, or your CRM of choice. Keep your pipeline data current without manual entry.

🎯

Stakeholder Alignment

Generate a team digest of the call for internal stakeholders. Share findings with engineering, product, or marketing to ensure whole-company alignment.

Real-World Use Cases

📹 Post-Call Autopilot

Call ends, recording auto-uploads. Within 10 minutes, you have a transcript, action items, follow-up email, and updated CRM record. Focus on selling, not admin.

👥 Sales Team Handoff

Sales engineer hands off to account manager. Call Summary automatically pulls information and generates context briefing so the account manager can continue seamlessly.

🚨 Deal Risk Detection

Prospect goes quiet. Call Summary flags sentiment changes, unaddressed objections, and commitment hesitation from your last call. Flag risks before deals slip.

📋 Forecast Accuracy

Automatically extract deal stage signals from calls (budget confirmed, timeline set, stakeholder alignment). Feed insights into your forecast model for better predictions.
