Performance Report

Turn marketing data into actionable insights. Aggregate metrics across campaigns, channels, and timeframes with AI-powered analysis that identifies trends, celebrates wins, and flags opportunities for improvement. Get executive summaries for leadership, detailed performance breakdowns by segment, and prescriptive optimization recommendations backed by your actual data.

Core Capabilities

Multi-Channel Aggregation

Pull data from email, social, ads, website analytics, and CRM. Correlate metrics across channels to understand true impact of integrated campaigns and identify attribution patterns.

Smart Trend Detection

Automatically identify MoM and YoY trends in key metrics. Detect anomalies, seasonal patterns, and inflection points. Understand what's working, what's plateauing, and what needs intervention.

Competitive Analysis

Benchmark your performance against industry standards. Understand how your metrics stack up on engagement rates, conversion rates, ROI, and spend efficiency compared to your competitive set.

Prescriptive Recommendations

AI-generated optimization suggestions backed by your data. Specific, actionable advice: reallocate budget here, test this creative angle, expand to this audience segment. Ranked by expected impact.

Executive Summaries

One-pager reports for board meetings and leadership reviews. Key metrics highlighted, wins celebrated, risks flagged, next quarter priorities outlined. Perfect for stakeholder communication.

Segment Breakdowns

Analyze performance by campaign, audience segment, geography, device, and more. Identify which segments drive revenue, which show promise, and which need re-evaluation or pausing.
