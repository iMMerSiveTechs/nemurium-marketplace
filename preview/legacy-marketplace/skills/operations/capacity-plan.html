Capacity Plan

Do you have enough people? Should you hire or rebalance? Analyze workload, forecast utilization, and make hire-vs-deprioritize decisions with data instead of gut feel. Build plans that scale without constantly firefighting.

Core Capabilities

What you can do with Capacity Plan

📊

Workload Analysis

Map all projects and tasks. Volume, complexity, owner, deadline. See where all hours go. Identify overload early.

📈

Utilization Forecasting

Current load + roadmap = utilization 6 months out. Will you be 70% full or 140%? Plan accordingly.

💼

Skill-Based Allocation

Not all engineers are equal. Match project needs to team skills. Identify gaps and hiring priorities.

💰

Hire-vs-Deprioritize Modeling

Cost of new headcount vs delay on feature X. Run scenarios. What if we hired 2 people? Promoted someone? Outsourced Y?

🎯

Burnout Early Warning

Track hours per person per week. Flag when someone hits 50+ sustained hours. Intervene before they leave.

📆

Seasonal/Cyclical Planning

Some teams peak in Q4, quiet in Q2. Capacity plan adjusts for your cycle. Hire/ramp/contract at the right time.

Perfect For

Annual Budget Cycle

Build hiring headcount request on data. Show CFO exactly where the 3 new roles go. Approval in days, not weeks.

Scaling Pains

Hypergrowth means resource chaos. Capacity plan keeps org aligned as you scale. Track FTE needs vs reality.

Retention & Burnout

See who's overloaded before exit interviews. Rebalance workload, hire support, offer sabbaticals. Act early.

Cross-Team Prioritization

Multiple teams competing for shared resources. Capacity plan shows what fits. Kill "everything is priority one."
