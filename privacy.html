Privacy notice

Not active yet. This is the proposed privacy notice for the GlassGraph launch list. The signup stays closed until this notice is approved and made effective.

What the launch list is for

When it opens, the list will send GlassGraph Studio launch news and occasional product updates to people who ask to receive them.

What the planned signup will collect

The planned signup will collect your email address, the version of the consent notice you accepted, when you signed up, and limited technical information needed to protect the form from abuse.

What it will not collect

The launch list is not designed to collect the content of your GlassGraph boards. Your boards stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them through a separate product feature.

How your email will be used

The planned use is limited to sending the launch and product news you asked for, maintaining a record of your request, and protecting the list from spam or misuse.

Your choices

Every launch-list email will include a way to unsubscribe. You will also be able to ask about your email or the list by writing to immersivetechs@nemurium.com.

Before this becomes active

NEMURIUM will publish the effective version of this notice, the sender details, and the required contact information before collecting launch-list email addresses.
