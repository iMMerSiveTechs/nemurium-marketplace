Email Sequence

Build high-converting email sequences that nurture leads into customers. Design multi-email drip campaigns with intelligent timing, behavioral branching, and exit conditions. Test subject lines and copy variations, set up automated A/B tests, and let AI optimize each email for opens, clicks, and conversions. From welcome series to cart abandonment to loyalty campaigns—all template-driven and ready to deploy.

Core Capabilities

Multi-Email Sequences

Generate complete 5-15 email sequences with strategic spacing. Welcome series, nurture sequences, re-engagement campaigns, win-back sequences—all templated and ready to customize with your messaging.

Intelligent Timing

Auto-calculate optimal send times based on subscriber timezone, behavior patterns, and engagement history. Space emails to maximize open rates and prevent list fatigue while maintaining campaign momentum.

Behavioral Branching

Create conditional email paths based on subscriber actions: opened, clicked, purchased, or took no action. Route subscribers to different sequences automatically and increase relevance through personalized journeys.

Subject Line Optimization

Generate multiple subject line variations with proven emotional triggers. Includes curiosity gaps, urgency, social proof, and personalization tokens. Built-in preview text optimization for mobile displays.

A/B Testing Framework

Automated testing of subject lines, body copy, CTAs, and send times. Intelligent segment splitting ensures statistical significance. Auto-scale winning variations and continuously improve performance metrics.

Exit Condition Rules

Smart unsubscribe prevention and engagement recovery. Set up rules to pause sequences for active converters, re-route disengaged subscribers, or trigger alternative messaging before someone leaves.

Real-World Use Cases

🎯

SaaS Onboarding Funnel

8-email sequence from signup through activation. Behavioral branching routes users based on product use: activated users get upgrade series, inactive users get help/troubleshooting sequence, churn risk users get VIP support offers.

🛒

E-Commerce Cart Abandonment

3-email cart recovery sequence with escalating urgency and incentives. First email is product reminder, second adds social proof/reviews, third includes limited-time discount. Branching handles completed purchases and shows different post-purchase sequences.

📚

Lead Nurturing Sequence

12-email educational drip campaign that builds trust and positions your company as thought leader. Different sequences for different industries/use cases based on signup data. Built-in case study and demo requests at optimal points.

💎

VIP Win-Back Campaign

6-email sequence targeting churned customers with special re-engagement offers. High-value personalization with their purchase history. Branching detects if they've already re-purchased and stops further emails automatically.
