NDA Triage

Instantly classify NDAs into GREEN/YELLOW/RED risk buckets. Identify non-solicits, non-competes, missing carveouts, and termination risks in seconds.

Core Capabilities

📊

Risk Classification

Automatically classify NDAs into GREEN (standard), YELLOW (requires review), RED (escalate immediately). Make triage decisions in seconds, not hours.

🚫

Non-Solicitation Analysis

Detect non-solicitation provisions. Identify scope gaps and carveouts. Flag geographical and temporal limits that may conflict with business plans.

⛔

Non-Compete Detection

Extract non-compete terms, duration, territory, and business line restrictions. Assess enforceability concerns and operational impact.

🔑

Carveout Identification

Find missing carveouts for general knowledge, public domain info, and prior company work. Flag gaps that could expose you to liability.

⏳

Termination & Duration

Extract renewal, termination, and perpetual confidentiality terms. Flag indefinite survival clauses that may outlive the business relationship.

🎯

Counterparty Risk

Assess counterparty obligation symmetry. Flag one-sided NDAs and restrictive definitions that limit your business flexibility.

Common Use Cases

🤝 Vendor Onboarding

New vendors, partners, and consultants often present NDAs as boilerplate. Triage them instantly. Flag non-competes that would conflict with existing hires or planned roles.

🏢 Strategic Discussions

Before starting partnership or acquisition talks, receive an NDA. Classify it and flag deal-breaker terms before your team signs and gives away information.

👥 Hire & Separation

Evaluate non-competes and non-solicits before hiring an executive. Understand restrictions that may limit the hire's value or create enforceability risk.

📋 Due Diligence

Target company has a book of partner NDAs. Batch-process them to find non-competes that might restrict post-acquisition plans or require renegotiation.
