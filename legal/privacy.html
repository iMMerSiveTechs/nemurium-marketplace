Proposed product-content boundary

GlassGraph is intended to be local-first. Before any policy becomes effective, the owner must verify precisely what remains on a customer’s Mac, what may leave it through an export or optional connection, and what data is needed to operate or improve the product. This draft does not make a final claim about board content, assistant features, diagnostics, or sharing behavior.
