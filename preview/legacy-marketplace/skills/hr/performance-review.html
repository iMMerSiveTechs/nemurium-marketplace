SKILL

Performance Review

Run continuous, data-driven performance cycles with real-time feedback, calibration facilitation, and behavioral insights. Move beyond annual reviews to create a culture of feedback and growth.

Core Capabilities

Review Cycles & Templates

Design annual, mid-year, and continuous feedback cycles. Create role-specific review templates with competency rubrics, goal reviews, and 360-degree sections.

Self-Assessment Tools

Guide employees to reflect on accomplishments, challenges, and growth. Gather self-perception data that feeds into manager reviews and calibration conversations.

Manager Review Forms

Structured forms with competency scoring, behavioral anchors, and written feedback prompts. Standardize ratings and reduce bias in manager assessments.

360 Feedback

Collect peer, direct report, and skip-level feedback. Anonymize responses to encourage candor. Compare self-perception to others' views for development insights.

Calibration Meetings

Facilitate manager panels to align ratings, identify high performers and flight risks, and discuss development. Use data to reach consensus and reduce inconsistency.

Performance Analytics

Analyze rating distributions, identify potential rating bias, track performance trends by function and level. Spot top talent, succession candidates, and PIP requirements.

Common Use Cases

Annual Performance Cycles

Run synchronized review periods across the organization. Collect feedback, facilitate calibration, and feed results into comp and promotion decisions.

Continuous Performance Management

Move beyond annual reviews with mid-year check-ins, real-time feedback, and ongoing goal tracking. Catch performance issues early and celebrate wins frequently.

Promotion & Succession Planning

Use calibration data to identify ready-now and future leaders. Base promotion decisions on documented performance and competency alignment with next-level expectations.

Diversity & Equitable Feedback

Reduce bias in performance ratings through structured rubrics and calibration. Analyze whether feedback differs by gender, race, or tenure for similar performance.
