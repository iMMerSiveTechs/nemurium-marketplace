Why GlassGraph

Some work is too connected for a list.

When a project has too many moving pieces, ordinary notes and task lists hide the relationships that matter most.

GlassGraph turns scattered pieces into a clear picture, so you can understand the work before deciding what to change. NEMURIUM is the independent brand behind GlassGraph Studio.

01

Make the whole picture visible

Place ideas, tasks, people, tools, and decisions on one board so you can see how the parts fit together.

02

Follow what depends on what

Trace relationships and dependencies, spot missing pieces, and understand where one decision affects everything else.

03

Stay in control of your work

GlassGraph is designed to keep your board on your Mac by default. You choose when to export it, share it, or connect another tool.

What you can do

Map the work. Explore it. Explain it.

GlassGraph keeps the big picture and the practical details together, so your map can grow with the work instead of becoming another forgotten diagram.

01

Build a living visual map

Add what matters, connect related pieces, group them, move them, and reshape the board as your understanding changes.

02

Look from different angles

Move from the big picture to ordered steps, detailed comparisons, and focused exploration without splitting your thinking across separate apps.

03

Start with a useful structure

Begin with a ready-made visual structure, then adapt the board to your project, system, or way of thinking.

04

Keep and carry your work

GlassGraph is designed to save your board locally while you work. Export a portable copy whenever you want to share, archive, or move it.

AI on your terms

Let an agent see the board you choose.

GlassGraph keeps your board on your Mac by default. A visual-sharing beta for compatible AI assistants, using MCP, is coming soon—and it stays off until you turn it on. Sharing stops when you switch boards, turn off agent access, or close GlassGraph.

Share only when you choose

Turn visual access on for the current board when you want an agent to explain, review, or showcase your work.

Your desktop stays private

The agent receives a picture of the visible board canvas—not your desktop, other windows, source files, or hidden content.

You choose the approval level

Suggestions wait for review by default. Turn on board-building for the open board to let routine map edits apply without approving every node; anything outside the board still stops for review.

Copilot Beta and guided presentations are coming soon. We are developing a lightweight built-in assistant and guided board presentations to help you understand and share the story behind a map.
