Current prelaunch status

GlassGraph Studio does not currently accept payment or provide a checkout. The recorded payment provider candidate is Stripe, but no live price, payment link, subscription, customer portal, entitlement, receipt flow, or refund workflow is verified by this draft.

Planned launch offer

The current release record describes a planned seven-day free trial with no card required, for use on up to two devices. It describes a planned $10 USD monthly subscription after the trial only if the person actively chooses to subscribe. It records no annual plan. These are planned candidate facts, not active commercial terms.

Trial and renewal

The planned no-card trial would not automatically become a paid subscription. Before any subscription can open, the final billing flow must clearly state the price, currency, taxes if applicable, charge timing, renewal behavior, and how a person can cancel. Those implementation and disclosure details remain approval_required.

Cancellation

The actual cancellation path is approval_required. Before launch, it must be implemented, tested, and described in plain language in the final billing experience. This draft does not claim a customer portal, account page, email path, cancellation timing, proration rule, or continued-access rule exists.

Proposed refund handling

The existing release contract records a proposed request window of seven days from each charge, except fraud or abuse and subject to mandatory legal rights and payment-processor handling. This is not an automatic-refund promise. The final policy must specify the operational request route, review process, timing, any exceptions, and how the chosen payment processor will carry out an approved refund.

Taxes, receipts, and payment records

Tax treatment, merchant descriptor, receipt sender, invoice behavior, records, charge disputes, and customer support escalation are not decided here. The founder must approve them after the live payment configuration and commerce workflow are verified.

What must happen before publication

  • Configure and test the actual purchase, entitlement, cancellation, and refund paths.
  • Confirm the final customer disclosures against the approved payment provider configuration.
  • Replace every DRAFT and approval_required marker only after written founder approval and appropriate legal review.
