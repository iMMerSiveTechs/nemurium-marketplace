SKILL

Org Planning

Design your organization from the ground up with data-driven headcount planning, role mapping, reporting structures, and span-of-control analysis. Align org design with strategy and optimize talent spend.

Core Capabilities

Headcount Planning

Build multi-year headcount forecasts by department, level, and location. Align hiring with revenue growth, product roadmap, and budget constraints. Model different scaling scenarios.

Org Design

Visualize reporting structures, span of control, and organizational layers. Test flatter designs vs. hierarchical models. Optimize for communication, accountability, and agility.

Role Architecture

Define levels (IC1 through E5), competencies by level, and title standards. Build a consistent role framework that enables internal mobility and clear career pathing.

Succession Planning

Map critical roles, identify successors, and build development plans for high-potential talent. Document leadership bench strength and readiness for key positions.

Organizational Analytics

Analyze headcount distribution, gender/race/age representation by level, span of control, and skip-level metrics. Identify bottlenecks and diversity gaps.

Scenario Modeling

Model impact of growth, M&A, restructuring, or market downturns on org structure. Forecast comp spend, hiring velocity, and team composition changes.

Common Use Cases

Strategic Planning & Board Reporting

Build multi-year talent plans that align with revenue targets and strategic initiatives. Show board the path to scale and associated talent cost implications.

Departmental Restructuring

Test org changes before executing. Model span of control, career progression, and cross-team collaboration impacts. Plan communication and transition carefully.

M&A Integration

Map legacy orgs against new org design. Plan role consolidations, identify redundancies, and manage sensitive eliminations and relocations.

Diversity & Inclusion Planning

Track representation by department, level, and function. Set hiring targets and measure progress toward diversity goals aligned with org growth.
