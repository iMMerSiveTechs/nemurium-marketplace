Typical Use Cases

Prepare comprehensive briefings before first customer meetings

Walk into every call informed. Know their recent news, leadership, funding stage, and technology choices before you say hello. Build credibility and relevance instantly.

Identify buying signals through organizational changes

New CEO or SVP means new priorities. New hiring in specific departments signals investment areas. Funding announcements mean budget to deploy. Spot opportunities faster.

Validate ICP fit before investing in sales cycles

Research technology stack, funding stage, and market positioning before engaging. Disqualify non-fits early and focus your team on high-probability accounts.

Build account strategies with multi-threaded insights

Connect insights across news, leadership, hiring, and customers to craft positioning. Find the narrative that resonates with their current priorities and challenges.
