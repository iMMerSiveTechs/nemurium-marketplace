Find Discussions

Locate relevant conversations about specific topics across your Slack workspace. Find stakeholders, understand context, and gather information from discussions without manually scanning channels.

Capabilities

🎯
Topic-Based Discovery
Search for discussions by topic, project, or keyword. Find all mentions and conversations related to what you're looking for.
👥
Stakeholder Identification
Discover who is involved in discussions about specific topics. Identify key decision makers, contributors, and interested parties.
🗺️
Cross-Channel Navigation
Find discussions spread across multiple channels. Get a complete picture of conversations without knowing which channels to check.
📌
Context Aggregation
Collect related messages and threads into a cohesive context summary. Understand the full story without jumping between channels.
⏱️
Timeline View
See how discussions evolved over time. Track decisions, debates, and changes in perspective chronologically.
🔗
Deep Link Generation
Generate shareable links to specific discussions and thread timestamps for easy reference and collaboration.

Use Cases

Onboarding & Context Building
New team members can quickly find discussions about their team, projects, and processes to understand organizational context and history.
Meeting Preparation
Before important meetings, find all relevant discussions, decisions, and stakeholder perspectives to come prepared and informed.
Root Cause Analysis
Investigate incidents or problems by finding all related discussions, decisions that led to the issue, and lessons learned.
Project & Initiative Discovery
Learn about projects across the organization by finding and reading their Slack discussions, planning, and execution conversations.
