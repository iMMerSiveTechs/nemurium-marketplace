Memory Management

Master a two-tier memory system. Keep working memory sharp, archive long-term context, and decode shorthand at high speed. Build a searchable knowledge base that grows with your work.

Working Memory

Keep your active context sharp and current. Fast lookup, automatic expiration, and weekly refresh cycles.

📚

Long-Term Archive

Preserve important context and decisions for future reference. Searchable, tagged, and always available.

🔤

Shorthand Decoding

Learn custom abbreviations and shorthand. System auto-expands and explains as you work.

Knowledge Base Search

Full-text search across all archived context. Find decisions, precedents, and past solutions instantly.

Automatically tag and categorize context by theme, person, project, or custom dimension.

Memory Analytics

Understand what you remember best, common questions, and knowledge gaps in your system.
