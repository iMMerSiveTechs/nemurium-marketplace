Channel Digest

Stay informed without information overload. Automatically summarize activity across multiple Slack channels, highlighting key decisions, important discussions, and action items in a daily or weekly digest.

Capabilities

🎯
Multi-Channel Aggregation
Combine activity from multiple channels into a single cohesive summary. Filter by relevance, priority, and frequency.
⚡
Key Decision Extraction
Identify and highlight decisions made across channels, including context, stakeholders, and rationale for quick reference.
✅
Action Item Detection
Automatically extract action items, assignments, and deadlines from conversations to stay on top of commitments.
📊
Sentiment & Priority Analysis
Understand emotional tone and urgency level of discussions to focus on what matters most.
📅
Flexible Scheduling
Choose daily, weekly, or custom digest schedules. Control timing, format, and distribution channels.
🎨
Custom Formatting
Receive digests in formats optimized for reading—markdown, HTML, or plain text delivered to Slack or email.

Use Cases

Executive Visibility
Give leadership a weekly digest of key decisions, risks, and progress across engineering, product, and operations without requiring them to monitor channels directly.
Cross-functional Awareness
Help teams stay informed about developments in other departments that affect their work, reducing silos and improving coordination.
Time Zone Synchronization
For distributed teams, provide a comprehensive catch-up digest for team members logging in across different time zones.
On-Call & Incident Response
Keep on-call engineers and incident commanders informed about relevant discussions and decisions during their shift.
