SKILL

Standup Generator

No more "uh... what did I do yesterday?" Automatically generate meaningful standup updates from your commits, pull requests, and ticket activity. Share what you accomplished, not scrambled notes.

Core Capabilities

📊

Activity Aggregation

Pull commits, PRs merged, tickets completed, code reviews performed. Synthesize scattered activity into coherent narrative. Include links to work products. Data-driven standup instead of memory.

✍️

Natural Language Generation

Convert raw activity into well-formatted standup updates. "Merged 3 PRs, closed 2 bugs" becomes "Completed payment processing feature and resolved critical caching issues." Professional presentation.

📋

Template Customization

Customize for your team's format. Did/doing/blockers. Focus areas by category. Metric highlights. Adapt to standup style without regenerating raw data.

🔗

Cross-Tool Integration

Connect GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear. Pull complete picture of work across systems. Engineer contributes to frontend, backend, and infrastructure? All activities included.

⏰

Scheduled Generation

Daily standup reports automatically generated at 8:55 AM. Ready to share or tweak. Stop spending Friday afternoon writing "what did I do this week?"

📈

Progress Tracking

Weekly summaries showing velocity. Track code volume, review count, tickets closed. Personal metrics over time. Visibility into individual productivity without surveillance.

Real-World Applications

📅 Daily Team Standup

Engineering team of 8 runs 15-minute daily standup. Instead of "um what did I do," everyone has clear updates. Meetings run on time. Focus on blockers instead of activity recap.

📝 Status Reports

Manager needs status for leadership. Generator pulls last week's activity. "Completed 18 PRs, reviewed code for team, shipped 2 critical bugs." Takes 30 seconds, data-backed.

👥 Performance Reviews

Preparation for quarterly reviews. Generator shows complete work history with metrics. Concrete evidence of contributions. Eliminates recency bias where recent work overshadows previous impact.

🤝 Cross-Team Updates

Frontend team communicates progress to backend team. Weekly standup summaries formatted for stakeholders. Clear ownership and progress visibility across dependencies.
