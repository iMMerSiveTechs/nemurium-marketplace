Common Use Cases

🚀 Product Launch

New feature uses customer data differently. Run it through compliance check. Get approval roadmap, identify data residency requirements, and timeline for GDPR/CCPA compliance.

🌍 Geographic Expansion

Moving to a new country? Check compliance requirements for that jurisdiction. Identify local data privacy laws, industry regulations, and operational requirements before market entry.

🏥 Regulated Industry Entry

Entering healthcare, fintech, or insurance? Map HIPAA, SOX, or sector-specific compliance requirements. Build your compliance plan before engineering starts.

🤝 Partnership & Integration

Third-party integration handles sensitive data? Verify compliance implications. Identify data handling requirements, vendor audit criteria, and contractual obligations.
