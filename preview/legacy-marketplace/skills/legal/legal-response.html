Legal Response

Craft professional, legally-sound responses to DSRs, litigation holds, vendor questions, and NDA requests. Built-in escalation checks flag when leadership needs to weigh in.

Core Capabilities

📝

Response Templates

Input the request type (DSR, litigation hold, vendor question). Get a legally-sound draft response customized to your company and the specific request.

🔍

Request Classification

Automatically categorize incoming legal requests. Identify DSRs, CCPA/GDPR rights requests, litigation holds, and third-party inquiries. Route to appropriate team.

⚠️

Escalation Detection

Flag responses that require leadership input. Multi-jurisdictional compliance, settlement authority, precedent-setting decisions—escalate before sending.

✉️

NDA Request Handler

Third party requests an NDA. Evaluate their request. Suggest whether to use your standard, negotiate key terms, or decline. Include draft redlines if needed.

⏰

Deadline Tracking

Extract and track response deadlines from incoming requests. Flag when deadlines are approaching. Prevent missed compliance deadlines.

📋

Audit Trail

Log all requests and responses. Maintain audit trail for regulatory inquiry. Document decision-making process and escalation chain.

Common Use Cases

📄 Data Subject Rights (DSR)

GDPR or CCPA subject requests their data or demands deletion. Get response template that acknowledges request, explains verification process, and commits to timeline.

⚖️ Litigation Hold

Threatened lawsuit. Need to issue litigation hold. Draft notice that preserves evidence, sets expectations, and documents compliance. Route to business leaders.

🤝 Vendor Inquiry

Third-party vendor asks compliance questions. Integration questions about data handling. Draft response that addresses their concerns without over-committing.

📋 NDA Request

Business development team wants to share confidential info but counterparty wants an NDA. Evaluate their request. Use your standard if possible; negotiate if not.
