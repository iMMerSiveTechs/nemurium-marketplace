Skill

Customer Escalation

Package escalations for engineering, product, and leadership with full context. Auto-build executive summaries, attach evidence, set severity levels, and notify the right stakeholders—instantly.

Key Capabilities

📋

Executive Summary

Auto-generate 1-page summaries of the issue: what happened, customer impact, expected resolution, and next steps.

🔍

Evidence Attachment

Gather logs, screenshots, error traces, and customer feedback. Compile into a dossier with context.

🎯

Severity & SLA Marking

Auto-assess severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low) and assign SLA timers. Flag time-sensitive escalations.

📧

Smart Routing

Route to engineering, product, or leadership based on issue type. Notify via email, Slack, or ticketing system.

🔗

Cross-Team Alignment

Link escalations to Jira issues, product roadmap items, and known bugs. Keep teams in sync.

📊

Escalation Analytics

Track escalation volume, resolution time, and root causes. Spot systemic issues early.

Common Use Cases

Critical Production Incidents

Customer down. P1 escalation sent to engineering and leadership in seconds with full logs and impact assessment.

Enterprise Customer Churn Risk

VIP customer threatening to leave. Flag to CSM and product leadership with retention strategy and talking points.

Product Feedback at Scale

Multiple customers requesting a feature. Auto-escalate patterns to product team with demand signals and use cases.

Compliance & Legal Issues

Potential data breach or legal issue. Escalate to legal/security with full evidence, timeline, and remediation steps.
