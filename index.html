Governance membrane

Every meaningful change asks first

One membrane governs every AI authoring rail — an external MCP client today, and the built-in Copilot when it ships. AI writes are proposal-first, bound to the exact board bytes they were made against, and reviewed before anything mutates. Your own edits are direct.

01

Propose

An AI drafts a change, bound to the exact board bytes it was made against.

02

Validate

Schema, capability, capacity, and expiry checks run before review.

03

Review

You approve, edit, or deny in an app-owned review surface.

04

Apply

Approved changes commit atomically to a new revision with a receipt.

05

Record

The receipt pins the board bytes before and after, and that a human decided.

Deny = no mutation. A denied proposal leaves the board untouched and records the decision. A proposal that no longer matches the board it was made against is rejected rather than merged. Verified behaviour, not a promise.
