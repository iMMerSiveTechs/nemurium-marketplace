SKILL

Forecast

Build accurate forecasts that executives trust. Weighted probability models, best/likely/worst case scenarios, gap analysis, and dynamic adjustments based on deal intelligence.

📉

What You Can Do

🎯

Weighted Pipeline Analysis

Apply realistic win probabilities to each deal based on stage, activity, and historical conversion rates. Replace gut feel with data-driven forecasts.

📊

Best/Likely/Worst Scenarios

Model three outcomes for your forecast. See the upside opportunity, realistic projection, and downside risk. Plan for all scenarios.

🔍

Gap Analysis & Action Planning

If you're short of quota, see exactly which deals need to be pulled forward or which new business is required. Prioritize actions that close the gap.

📈

Rolling 13-Week View

Forecast doesn't reset quarterly. Rolling 13-week view shows trends, seasonality, and pipeline health. Plan beyond the month-end cliff.

⚠️

Deal-Level Risk Assessment

Flag deals with low activity, stalled progress, or key person risks. Understand what could derail your forecast and fix it proactively.

📋

Forecast vs. Actual Tracking

Compare your forecast to actual close rates. Improve your model over time. Get more accurate with every forecast cycle.

Real-World Use Cases

📊 Board Reporting

Build a credible forecast with best/likely/worst scenarios. Present to the board with confidence, backed by weighted pipeline analysis and deal-level intelligence.

🎯 Quota Planning

Using historical close rates and seasonal trends, forecast next quarter's realistic revenue. Set quotas and targets based on data, not politics.

💰 Cash Flow Projection

Finance needs to know cash inflows. Use deal-level timing, probability, and realistic close dates to forecast cash arrival. Support the business plan with confidence.

🚨 Mid-Quarter Adjustments

It's the 15th of the month. You're on track or off track? Quickly re-forecast based on new deal information, closures, and slippages. Adjust strategy in real time.
