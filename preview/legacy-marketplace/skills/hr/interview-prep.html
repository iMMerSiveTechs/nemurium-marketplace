SKILL

Interview Prep

Structure every interview with competency-based frameworks, standardized scorecards, and behavioral rubrics. Reduce bias, improve consistency, and build a predictive hiring process that scales with your organization.

Core Capabilities

Competency Mapping

Define job-specific competencies, behavioral anchors, and proficiency levels. Create reusable competency libraries that ensure every interviewer is aligned on what excellence looks like for the role.

Interview Plans

Generate structured interview agendas with topic sequences, time allocations, and suggested questions. Ensure consistent candidate experience across all interview rounds.

Scoring Rubrics

Build detailed evaluation rubrics with clear performance bands (exemplary, proficient, developing, below expectation). Make scoring objective and defensible.

Behavioral Questions

Access a curated library of STAR-format behavioral questions by competency. Prompt interviewers to dig deeper and validate real-world capability.

Debrief Facilitation

Run calibration sessions where your team scores candidates together, discusses findings, and reaches consensus. Minimize individual bias through structured dialogue.

Hiring Analytics

Track time-to-hire, interview completion rates, inter-rater reliability, and offer acceptance rates. Spot bottlenecks and optimize your talent pipeline.

Common Use Cases

High-Volume Recruiting

Standardize your college recruiting, customer service, and retail hiring with repeatable competency scorecards. Train new hiring managers in minutes, not weeks.

Senior Hires & Board-Level Roles

Create executive assessment frameworks with domain expertise panels. Document decision rationale and leadership fit criteria for board visibility.

DEI & Bias Reduction

Use structured questions and blind scoring to reduce hiring bias. Track diversity metrics by role and interview round to identify where gaps emerge.

Distributed Teams

Conduct asynchronous video interviews, guided self-assessments, and remote panel discussions. Coordinate global hiring across time zones without losing quality.
